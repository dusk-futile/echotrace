# Idea Universe

> Turn a raw thought into a structured map of possibilities.

## What It Does

Idea Universe accepts rough voice transcripts, incomplete notes, contradictory thoughts, personal goals, niche interests, essay ideas, research questions, projects, products, technical concepts, nonprofit ideas, creative work, and personal decisions.

It organizes the raw input and explores relevant:

- ideas;
- audiences;
- formats;
- fields;
- analogies;
- technologies;
- opportunities;
- personal connections;
- examples;
- research topics;
- and second-order possibilities.

Idea Universe aims to create the broadest useful map of relevant possibilities without turning the response into a random list.

## What Makes It Different

Idea Universe:

- expands before evaluating;
- uses personal context when relevant;
- searches for niche and unexpected connections;
- clusters ideas into distinct direction families;
- distinguishes user input from interpretation and research;
- does not force an early recommendation;
- does not issue Kill, Pivot, Test, or Build decisions.

Cold Review evaluates a defined idea. Idea Universe helps discover what an unfinished idea could become.

## How to Use

1. Open `PROMPT.md`.
2. Copy the complete prompt.
3. Paste it into an AI chat.
4. Explain your thought naturally through text, rough notes, or voice transcription.
5. Continue adding context until the AI has enough information.
6. Ask for the complete Idea Universe when ready.

## Best Used For

- brainstorming essays and speeches;
- developing research questions;
- discovering project directions;
- connecting ideas to personal experiences;
- exploring niche interests;
- creating technical or creative concepts;
- finding alternative audiences and formats;
- combining several unfinished ideas;
- organizing a long voice-dump.

## Important Limitations

- results depend on the AI model;
- personal context must not be invented;
- browsing may be incomplete;
- external references may be inaccurate;
- the user should verify important research;
- it is a brainstorming tool, not an automatic final decision maker.
